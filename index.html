Our Story

The Art of
Small Details.

"A meal is not just nourishment — it is memory, mood, and a moment frozen in time."

Miniature was born from an obsession with the details others overlook — the way candlelight bends across a handmade plate, the perfect temperature of a slow-braised sauce, the texture of linen against fingertips. We don't serve food. We architect experiences.

Nestled in the heart of Narsinghpur, our space is a sanctuary from the ordinary — designed for those who believe that where you dine is as important as what you dine on.

FAQ

Common
Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit to Miniature.

Do I need a reservation? +
We strongly recommend booking in advance, especially for weekends and special occasions. Walk-ins are welcome subject to availability, but we can rarely guarantee immediate seating without a reservation.
What is your dress code? +
We encourage smart casual to formal attire. We want every guest to feel they're part of an elevated experience — but we don't turn anyone away for their clothing choices.
Do you cater to dietary requirements? +
Absolutely. Our kitchen accommodates vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and allergy-specific menus. Please inform us at the time of booking or at least 24 hours in advance for special dietary arrangements.
Can I book a private dining room? +
Yes — our two private dining rooms can host 6–20 guests. They are available for anniversaries, corporate events, proposals, and exclusive tastings. Please contact us directly to enquire.
Is the Chef's Table experience available every day? +
The Chef's Table is available on select evenings — typically Thursday through Saturday. It accommodates just 4 guests and must be booked at least a week in advance. Contact us to check availability.
Is there parking available? +
Yes, we have complimentary valet parking for all our guests. We also have ample self-parking nearby for those who prefer it.
